What to Do When the Power Comes Back On

These instructions are for people at home who experience a power cut. Follow these steps when electricity returns to help protect yourself and your electrical appliances.

What You Need

  • A working torch or flashlight.
  • Access to your electrical switches, if safe.
  • The contact details of a qualified electrician.

Steps

  1. Wait until the electricity supply appears stable before switching appliances back on.
  2. Check for unusual smells, sparks, smoke, or damaged electrical wires without touching them.
  3. Do not touch exposed wires or electrical equipment that is wet or damaged.
  4. Unplug sensitive appliances if it is safe to do so and follow the manufacturer's instructions before reconnecting them.
  5. Turn appliances back on carefully, one at a time.
  6. If you notice an unusual electrical problem, stop using the affected equipment and contact a qualified electrician.

If Something Goes Wrong

If you see sparks or smoke, or smell burning, stay away from the affected equipment. Do not touch exposed wires or attempt repairs yourself. If there is a fire or immediate danger, move to safety and contact the appropriate emergency service. Never assume that an electrical appliance is safe just because the power has returned.

Electrical equipment may use PPE when being handled by trained professionals. Leave electrical repairs to a qualified person.
